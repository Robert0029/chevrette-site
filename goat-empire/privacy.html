Privacy Policy — GOAT EMPIRE

Last updated: 5 November 2026

GOAT EMPIRE is a mobile game published by Robert Petrosyan (Chevrette), Gyumri, Armenia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: support@chevrette.shop.

1. Information we collect

  • Chevrette account (optional). If you sign in, we process your email address and name. With Google sign-in we receive your Google name, email and profile picture. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider. You can play as a guest without an account.
  • Game data. Your progress, coins, goats, boosters, hearts, stars and Chevrette Points, plus round records (round ID, level, start and end time, result) and your time-zone offset so that daily hearts refill at your local midnight.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive the purchase token, order ID and product, which we use to verify the purchase with Google and deliver the item. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Advertising. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information and ad interactions to show, measure and protect ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

2. How we use information

To run the game and your account, sync progress across devices, verify purchases, prevent cheating and fraud, award Chevrette Points, provide support and improve stability. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Service providers

We share data only with providers needed to operate the game: Supabase (database and authentication hosting), Google Play (payments and purchase verification), Google AdMob (ads) and Google Sign-In. Each processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Chevrette Points

Chevrette Points are a loyalty balance shared with your Chevrette account. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn, sold or exchanged for money.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep account and game data while your account exists. You can delete your Chevrette account and all GOAT EMPIRE data at any time in the game: Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also request deletion by email — see Delete your account. Guest progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

6. Security

All data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to game data is restricted so that each player can access only their own data.

7. Your choices and rights

You can play without an account, reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device's Google settings, and ask us for a copy, correction or deletion of your data by emailing support@chevrette.shop. We reply within 30 days.

8. Children

GOAT EMPIRE is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the game evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
