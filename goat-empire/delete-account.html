GOAT EMPIRE · Account & Data

Delete your account

GOAT EMPIRE uses your Chevrette account. You can permanently delete the account and all game data yourself, right in the game.

In the game

  1. Open GOAT EMPIRE and sign in.
  2. Open Settings (gear icon in the main menu).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with Delete forever.

What is deleted

  • Your Chevrette account (email, name, sign-in data)
  • All GOAT EMPIRE progress: levels, stars, coins, goats, boosters, hearts
  • Chevrette Points and round history
  • Records of purchases made in the game
Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Because the account is shared, it is also removed from other Chevrette apps. Google Play keeps its own receipts of your payments as required by law.

No access to the game?

Email support@chevrette.shop from the email address of your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Удаление аккаунта

В игре: Настройки → Удалить аккаунт → Удалить навсегда. Удаляются аккаунт Chevrette и все данные GOAT EMPIRE: прогресс, монеты, козы, бусты, сердца, Chevrette Points, история раундов и покупок. Удаление сразу и навсегда. Нет доступа к игре? Напишите на support@chevrette.shop с почты аккаунта — удалим в течение 30 дней.
