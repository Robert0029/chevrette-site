HOW IT PLAYS

Build your army. Hold the castle.

10 worlds to conquer

Fight through ten different worlds, each with its own landscapes, enemies and harder waves of UFO invaders.

A boss every 10th level

Every tenth level ends with a giant boss battle. Plan your defense, upgrade your goats and bring them down.

Goat warriors

Five goat classes, including Warrior, Fire, Electric and Sniper goats, plus premium and legendary goats like the Goat King. Mix them to build the perfect defense.

Star progression
