Chevrette — Account & Data

Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Chevrette account and all associated data directly from within the app — no separate request or waiting period needed.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Chevrette app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete permanently in the dialog that appears.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently and immediately removes:

  • Your profile (name, email, wallet address if provided)
  • Your points and Daily Reward history
  • Your forged star in the Galaxy
  • Your referral relationships and campaign participation records
This action cannot be undone. Once deleted, your account and data cannot be recovered, and you would need to sign up again as a new user if you wish to use Chevrette in the future.

Don't have access to the app anymore?

If you're unable to open the app (e.g. you no longer have a compatible device), email us at support@chevrette.shop from the email address associated with your account, and we'll process the deletion on your behalf.

For more detail on what data we collect and how it's used, see our Privacy Policy.
