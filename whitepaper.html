White Paper

Chevrette White Paper

Version 1.0 · Last updated October 23, 2026

1. Abstract

One minute a day. One country to carry. Chevrette turns a one-minute check-in, a daily word puzzle and a worldwide country race into a game the whole world plays together. Players join from dozens of countries, pick a team and help it climb the leaderboard. This paper explains how Chevrette works, why it is built this way, and where it is going.

2. The problem

Most apps compete for hours of attention. People are tired of that. Many also feel left out of online competitions, because the biggest communities always win. Chevrette asks a different question: what if a very short daily action could make you part of something larger, and what if small countries had a real chance to win?

3. The idea

Show up every day. It takes less than a minute, and it counts twice: once for you and once for your flag. Fair rules protect small groups from being crushed by big ones, and every month the race starts again from zero.

4. How the product works

  • Daily check-in and streaks. Open the app every 24 hours, collect points, keep your streak. A live countdown shows when the next check-in is ready.
  • Chevrette Code. One five-letter word puzzle a day, six attempts, with hints. A solved puzzle earns points.
  • Country Race. Choose your country. Each season lasts one month and starts at zero. The country with the highest average activity per player wins, and eligible players of the winning country get a bonus on daily points for the whole next month.
  • Chevrette Galaxy. Every player has one unique star with a name, sector coordinates and a creation date, on a living map of the community. Explore the galaxy and search for any star.
  • Invite friends. Share your link or code. Currently you earn 500 points for each friend who joins and confirms an email, and your friend starts with 50 bonus points.
  • Leaderboard, tasks and news. See who leads, complete simple community tasks and read updates inside the app.
  • Optional boost. Watching a few short ads can activate a 5x boost on your next claim. It is always optional.

5. Fair play by design

  • A country needs at least 30 active players to enter the ranking, so a handful of accounts cannot take the lead.
  • The winning bonus goes only to players who were active for at least 10 days in the season and still belong to the winning country.
  • The winner bonus does not count toward the score, so the race stays open every month.
  • The first country choice is free. After that you can change it once every 30 days.
  • Accounts that abuse the rules can be blocked.

6. Reward Milestones

Every in-app reward is cut in half as the community grows. It is 50 points today and becomes 25 when Chevrette reaches 50,000 players. It is halved again at 100,000, 500,000, 1,000,000, 2,000,000, 5,000,000 and 10,000,000 players, down to a minimum of 1 point per daily check-in. Amounts are rounded down to whole points. Early players collect at the highest rates. Points are in-app rewards. They are not money and have no guaranteed monetary value. (See the Roadmap page for the full table.)

7. Chevrette NFTs

Chevrette Genesis and Chevrette Premium are NFT collections on Ethereum, available on OpenSea. Premium is a limited collection of 222 pieces. Owning an NFT is optional and is not needed to play or to take part in the Country Race.

8. Vision: a global ecosystem

The app is the foundation. Over time we plan to add a premium store, digital products, premium membership, community rewards and exclusive features. A utility token is an idea we are exploring carefully, and its details will be announced publicly first. Plans are intentions, not promises.

9. Community

Chevrette grows in the open. Join us on X (@chevrette_net), Telegram (t.me/chevrette_network), YouTube (@ChevretteNetwork), TikTok (@chevrette_network), Medium (@chevrette.shop) and in our Facebook group Chevrette Network. The Google Play page is available in 16 languages.

10. Team and contact

Chevrette is built by a small independent team that listens to its players. Every update starts with feedback from the community. To reach us, use the Contact page on this website or write to us on X or Telegram.

11. Principles

  1. Simple first: a ten-second habit that fits any day.
  2. Fair to everyone, big country or small.
  3. Honest about what points are and what they are not.
  4. Built in public and shaped by player feedback.
