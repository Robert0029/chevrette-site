Five stages. One direction.

More than 5,300 registered players from over 60 countries. Season 1 of the Country Race is live. About 780 unique stars already shine in Chevrette Galaxy. The app is on Google Play and the store page speaks 16 languages. Two NFT collections, Genesis and Premium, are live on Ethereum.

Reward Milestones

The earlier you join, the more every day is worth.

Chevrette's daily reward is not fixed. It steps down as the community grows. Today a daily check-in gives 50 points. When we reach 50,000 players, it becomes 25. At every new milestone it is cut in half again, all the way to 10,000,000 players, and the same goes for every other in-app reward. Players who arrive early collect points at the highest rates.

  • The reward changes for everyone at the same moment, when the milestone is reached. No exceptions and no private deals.
  • Milestones count registered players. We will keep this page up to date as the community grows.
  • Every in-app reward is cut in half at each milestone: the daily check-in, the 7-day streak cycle, Chevrette Code, invites and community tasks. The table shows the daily check-in. Amounts are whole points, rounded down, and never lower than 1 point.
  • Points are in-app rewards inside Chevrette. They are not money and have no guaranteed monetary value.

The roadmap describes our current intentions, not guarantees. Plans can change as we learn from the community.
